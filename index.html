Night Owl Mocha Cocoa (Abiha's Method)

The perfect comfort and caffeine boost for a crime podcast night.

Ingredients (Single Serving)

  • 2 tbsp Cocoa Powder (Unsweetened)
  • 2.5 tbsp Sugar (or to taste)
  • 1/2 tsp Instant Coffee Powder (or 1 shot strong Espresso)
  • Pinch of Salt (enhances cocoa flavor)
  • 1 cup Milk (Dairy or Oat)
  • Dash of Vanilla Extract
  • Dash of Cinnamon (plus extra for topping)
  • Dash of Coconut Oil (adds richness)

Instructions

  1. Flavor the Milk: Combine the milk, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and coconut oil in a saucepan or sealed container. Heat until steaming hot (do not boil).
  2. Emulsify: Thoroughly stir or shake the hot mixture to fully incorporate the coconut oil and flavors.
  3. The Slurry: In your favorite mug, mix the cocoa powder, sugar, instant coffee powder, and salt.
  4. Make the Paste: Pour a small amount (approx. 2 tbsp) of the hot, flavored milk into the mug and mix vigorously to create a smooth, thick paste (dissolving the coffee/cocoa).
  5. Finish: Top up the mug with the remaining hot milk mixture. Stir well.
  6. Garnish: Finish with a dash of extra cinnamon on top. Serve immediately!
